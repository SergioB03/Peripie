# Peripie Pitch Script (two minutes, v2)

About 175 spoken words plus roughly 35 seconds of live waits the speaker cannot talk over. Rehearse with the phone in hand; the taps are listed under each beat. Judge Q&A, failure drills and the 6 am checklist are in [demo-day.md](../demo-day.md).

Slides (five, same look as the builder mockup): https://claude.ai/artifact/Gu3infzvexEQmrrWRwK5Lr — present from the browser or export PPTX/PDF from its Share menu. Cover during 0:00; "Two people need this" through 0:15; the phone for the two live beats; "How one question works" at 1:20; the builder at 1:45; "What's next" for the close.

## 0:00 – The problem (40 words)
I run a painting company. My customers call to ask one thing: do you have it? Some days I'm the customer who won't ask, especially with a big order. This is a counter you can walk up to from anywhere.

*Tap: Rosa's Bakery is already selected. Tap Enter shop. Let the greeting play. (About 10 seconds.)*

## 0:20 – The bakery (30 words)
*Tap the mic. Say, a little hesitant: "Do you still have any croissants today?" Wait for the answer: the next tray comes out of the oven in a few minutes.*

That's real stock, in the bakery's own words, and the stock room's record is right there under it. The clerk isn't allowed to guess; it has to check first.

## 0:50 – The list (25 words)
*Tap sourdough, conchas and croissants under What we carry. Tap Check my list. Let the summary play. Tap Take this with you and show the card.*

This is the one I'd use. One list, one answer, and I can take it in with me instead of asking three times.

## 1:20 – The paint store (45 words)
*Tap Leave shop, tap Southside Paint Supply, tap Enter. Ask: "Do you have rollers?" Wait: the supplier's truck comes Friday; usually back in about four days.*

Same code, different shop. "Usually about four days" comes from sixty days of shelf counts in Tiger Data. Gemini is the clerk, ElevenLabs is the voice, and nothing about the visitor is stored. No account, no cookie, no log of what you asked.

## 1:45 – The close (35 words)
Each shop is one settings file. The builder we're making next lets an owner edit it by dragging things around, and staff it: a free shop with one clerk, paid add-on agents. Thank you.

## Cuts if a judge interrupts, in order
1. The last sentence of the paint beat ("No account, no cookie, no log of what you asked."); keep "nothing about the visitor is stored."
2. The paint-store switch itself. Say instead, from the bakery: "The paint store runs on the same code, with a support agent that answers which primer to use."
3. The second sentence of the opening ("Some days I'm the customer who won't ask…"). Only if desperate; it is the line judges remember.

## If asked right after
Which bread is best for sandwiches? Do you have 20 conchas? What time do you close? Do you deliver? Each has a short, honest answer built in; the list is in demo-day.md.
